# /brag-slim plan: Maya Moves

**What it is:** a booking and back-office app that runs a solo home physiotherapist's practice in Dubai.
**For:** Dr. Maya Rao (DHA-licensed, one therapist, visits across Marina → Business Bay) and her patients.
**Angle:** it takes her from *"can I book?"* to *"you're booked"* with nothing for Maya to do. This matches the Lovable x Contra "Built It For Small Business" brief.
**Hook:** Maya's real photo from the site, with her three problems stated in one line: *Traffic. No-shows. WhatsApp phone tag.*

**Tone:** calm premium: warm, confident, not hype-y. Soft slides, long holds, no hard cuts.
**Format:** vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, 30.0 s.
